Hanzi Writer is a Chinese character writing trainer that helps you learn and practice writing Hanzì using correct stroke order.

Each character is split into individual strokes — draw them one by one on a traditional Tian Zi Ge grid and get instant feedback on accuracy.

Features:
- 7 built-in HSK character sets (HSK 1–6 plus Russian translations)
- Three practice modes: Learn (step-by-step with diminishing hints), Drill (review), and Quiz (no hints)
- Correct stroke order validation with mistake tolerance
- Import your own character lists from CSV or ZIP files
- Track your daily practice streak on the calendar
- Demo animations showing proper stroke order for new characters
- Works offline — no account or internet required
